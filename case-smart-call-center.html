AI Solution

Smart Call Center

AI-powered automation for incoming and outgoing calls, built for high-volume organisations

Smart Call Center is an AI-based solution for managing an organisation's incoming and outgoing calls. Unlike traditional call centers, where every interaction depends on a human operator, BINARIS automatically handles part of the call volume, surfaces the right information to agents in the moment, and analyses the quality of every conversation. It is built for organisations with high call volumes — from healthcare and insurance providers to online retailers, fintech, logistics, and urban service companies.

The Solution

Speech processing, NLP, and large language models combine to create an intelligent layer on top of the answering process.

Voice Bot

Intelligent Call Routing

Topic-based routing of each call to the most appropriate unit or expert.

Real-Time Answer Suggestions

Live guidance for operators, based on the caller's question and the organisation's own knowledge base.

Speech-to-Text / Text-to-Speech

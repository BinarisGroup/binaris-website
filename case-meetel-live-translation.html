Meetel — Video Calls with
Live Voice Translation

Speak naturally in your language while other participants hear the conversation in theirs.

Meetel brings live speech-to-speech translation into browser-based video calls. Each participant speaks naturally in their own language, while the other hears the conversation in theirs — without typing, sentence-by-sentence pauses or a human interpreter.

Conceptual representation of Meetel’s multilingual calling experience.
Challenges

When Language Gets in the Way

Cross-language video calls often depend on expensive interpreters, slow text-translation tools or carefully scheduled support. The result is a conversation that feels delayed, fragmented and less human.

Costly Interpreters

Human support is expensive and must be arranged in advance.

Text Breaks the Flow

Typing and reading translations interrupts natural dialogue.

Delays and Coordination

Scheduling reduces access to immediate, spontaneous communication.

A Translator Built
into the Conversation

Powered by OpenAI Realtime speech-to-speech technology, Meetel sits between participants like a live interpreter. It combines translated audio, captions and browser-based HD calling in one frictionless experience.
